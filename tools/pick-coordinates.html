InhaAssist Coordinate Picker

Team tool. Click a building in the list, then click the middle of its roof on the map.

Choose a building in the list
  1. Pick a building (red = no position yet, orange = rough guess).
  2. Switch to Satellite (top right of the map) and click the roof's center. Drag the pin to adjust.
  3. When finished, press Download buildings.js and replace data/buildings.js with it.
Progress is saved in this browser, so you can stop and continue later.
